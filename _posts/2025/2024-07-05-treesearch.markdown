We introduce parallel symbolic enumeration (PSE) for discovering interpretable mathematical expressions from limited data. A parallel symbolic regression network shares the evaluation of common subexpressions and uses GPU computation to search efficiently. Evaluation on more than 200 synthetic and experimental problem sets demonstrates improved equation-recovery accuracy and computational efficiency over baseline methods.
