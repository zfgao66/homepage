PhononBench evaluates the dynamical stability of 133,838 crystal structures from seven crystal generation models using MatterSim-accelerated phonon calculations. It complements thermodynamic screening with a phonon-based assessment and releases 32,995 structures that meet a strict full-Brillouin-zone stability threshold of -0.001 THz. An online service enables minute-level phonon predictions for user-provided structures.
