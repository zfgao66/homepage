In this work, we present PhononBench-MP40, a spectrum-resolved benchmark dataset of Materials Project-derived crystals for workflow-defined phonon stability. The dataset provides 46,899 completed records with paired stability labels and local phonopy spectra, including 16,683 stable and 30,216 unstable records.
