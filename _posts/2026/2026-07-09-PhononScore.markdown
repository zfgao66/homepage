In this work, we propose PhononScore, a phonon-aware scoring function for crystal generation that directly predicts a unified dynamical-stability score from crystal structures, enabling second-level stability-aware reranking without explicit phonon calculations. The online PhononScore platform is available at http://phononbench.cn/phononscore/.
