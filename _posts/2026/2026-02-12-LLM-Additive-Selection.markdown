Perovskite-R1 guides the selection of ethyl 2-aminopropanoate hydrochloride (EAH) as a precursor additive for inverted perovskite solar cells. Its carbonyl and ammonium groups interact with undercoordinated lead and iodide ions, combining defect passivation with crystallization control to improve film quality and suppress nonradiative recombination. The champion device reaches a power-conversion efficiency of 22.58%, compared with 20.06% for the control. Unencapsulated EAH-treated devices retain 95.1% of their initial efficiency after 1,368 hours of storage in nitrogen at 25°C, and 94.1% after 1,272 hours of thermal aging at 65°C, demonstrating an experimentally validated application of LLM-assisted additive selection.
