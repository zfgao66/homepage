Crystoria connects thermodynamic and dynamical stability across 2,649,640 inorganic crystal structures spanning 89 elements and 219 space groups. Combining standardized structural identities, machine-learning-accelerated relaxation, and phonon-based screening, it identifies 1,793,353 dynamically stable structures under the stated screening protocol. The open dataset supports physically informed crystal generation, stability prediction, and materials discovery.
