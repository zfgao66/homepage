In this paper, we introduce MattKeyBond, a bond-centric materials database that explicitly maps the local electronic landscape and bonding interactions of materials, and build bond-centric descriptors that bridge crystal structure and material properties with improved interpretability and generalizability, particularly when training data are scarce.
